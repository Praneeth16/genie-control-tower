The problem and why now

Two books spiked this year, and some of the collection pressure breaks the RBI contact rules

What is happening
  • Microfinance delinquency in Solapur reached 43.6% in May 2026, against about 9% for the rest of the book.
  • Commercial vehicle delinquency in Indore reached 26.0% in the same month. By August both had eased, to 17.8% and 16.1%, still about twice the rest of the book.
  • Managers build the worklist by hand from several systems, so nobody knows which accounts to work first.
  • A file can arrive short and nobody notices. The dialer's June file arrived 37 rows short.
Why now
  • The RBI's August 2022 circular on recovery agents forbids contact before 08:00 or after 19:00. The lender answers for its agencies.
  • Under the RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021, a borrower can take an unresolved complaint about recovery agents to the Ombudsman.
  • Stress raises collection pressure exactly where conduct breaches are most likely.

Today against tomorrow

From hand-built lists and late surprises to one list rebuilt every time data lands

Today

Found after the complaint

  • Each manager builds a list from the dialer, the field app, the loan system and the CRM.
  • A short or missing file lowers a report, and nobody can see why.
  • A conduct breach is found when the borrower complains.
  • Every action depends on whoever happens to check it.
Tomorrow

Applied before contact

  • One worklist, ranked by a score whose reasons are stored on every row.
  • Every file is checked against what the sender declared, on the day it lands.
  • Accounts with an open grievance are held, and accounts with conduct flags are recommended for a certified agency.
  • Every action needs a named approver, and the bank's controls can refuse it.
